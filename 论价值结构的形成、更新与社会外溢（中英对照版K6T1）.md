# 论价值结构的形成、更新与社会外溢：一个整合性分析框架

**On the Formation, Renewal and Social Spillover of Value Structure: An Integrative Analytical Framework**

邹稻然（Zou Daoran）[^1]

【摘要】本文提出一种整合性的分析框架，用以描述个体价值判断如何形成、如何转化为持续行动、如何在社会互动中产生外部效应，以及新的经验如何反过来更新已有的认知模型。文章确立两项基础理论前提：第一，持续存在的主体才可能继续产生新的第一人称经验；第二，语言及其他公共描述方式对于第一人称经验存在原则上的认识论限制。在此基础之上提出 “人生主线” 概念，将自我认识界定为主体对自身价值结构的持续建模过程。本文引入 “激励函数” 作为价值结构的理论抽象，剖析欲望、目标、预设与直觉在价值动态演化之中的功能，提出 “目标自我包含性” 作为评判长期稳定目标的分析标准。在社会维度，建构精神调节负荷分析变量，讨论物质条件 - 精神状态之间的 “相对脱钩” 现象及其社会分布差异；在认知维度，阐释新经验输入驱动认知模型迭代更新的内在机制。本文力图打通主体不可替代性、激励结构的运作与更新、精神负荷社会分布、认知模型结构性变迁的理论链条，该框架不预设先验的人生目的，旨在提供一套解析价值生成、行动选择与社会外部效应的分析工具。

[Abstract] This paper proposes an integrative analytical framework for describing how individual value judgments are formed, how they are transformed into sustained action, how they produce external effects in social interaction, and how new experience in turn updates existing cognitive models. The paper establishes two foundational theoretical premises: first, only a persisting subject can continue to generate new first-person experience; second, language and other public modes of description face a principled epistemic limit with respect to first-person experience. On this basis the paper introduces the concept of the "main line of a life" and defines self-knowledge as the subject's ongoing modeling of its own value structure. The paper introduces the "incentive function" as a theoretical abstraction of value structure, analyzes the functions of desire, goals, presuppositions, and intuition in the dynamic evolution of value, and proposes "goal self-containment" (a goal's definition includes the mode of action that sustains it) as an analytical criterion for evaluating long-term stable goals. On the social dimension, it constructs the analytical variable of mental regulatory load and discusses the "relative decoupling" between material conditions and mental states together with its differential social distribution; on the cognitive dimension, it explains the internal mechanism by which new informational input drives the iterative updating of cognitive models. The paper seeks to connect into one theoretical chain the irreplaceability of the subject, the operation and renewal of the incentive structure, the social distribution of mental load, and the structural transformation of cognitive models. The framework presupposes no a priori purpose of life; it aims to provide a set of analytical tools for the analysis of value generation, action choice, and social external effects.

【关键词】自我认识；价值判断；个体能动性；社会互动；认知模型

[Keywords] self-knowledge; value judgment; individual agency; social interaction; cognitive model

**正文 / Main Text**

**一、引言：价值、行动与社会的连接问题 / I. Introduction: Value, Action, and the Question of Social Connection**

人生意义的讨论通常围绕幸福、事业、关系、财富或社会贡献等对象展开。这些对象均可能具有重要价值，但它们共享一个结构特征：其实现结果原则上可以由不同主体获得或承接。一项职业可由他人接替，一项事业可由后来者延续，一项任务也可能由不同主体完成。因此，有必要区分两个不同的问题：一件事情是否具有价值，以及一件事情是否具有不可替代的完成主体。本文关注第二个问题，并以此为基础构建后续分析。

Discussions of the meaning of life usually revolve around objects such as happiness, career, relationships, wealth, or social contribution. These objects may all be of great value, but they share one structural feature: their realized outcomes can in principle be obtained or taken over by different subjects. A profession can be succeeded by someone else; a cause can be continued by those who come later; a task can likewise be completed by a different subject. It is therefore necessary to distinguish two different questions: whether something has value, and whether something has an irreplaceable completer. This paper focuses on the second question and builds its subsequent analysis on that basis.

与此同时，一个人的价值判断并非静止不变。新的经验、新的信息以及社会互动中的反馈，都可能改变一个人 “想要什么” 以及 “如何判断什么值得追求” 的整体结构。如果这一结构持续变化，那么我们就需要一套概念工具来解释这种变化的方向、条件和后果。更为重要的是，一个人的行动选择并非只影响自身。当个体依照自己的价值判断行动时，其行为会改变他人的资源条件、心理负荷和行动空间。因此，价值结构的研究不能止步于个体内部，还必须延伸到社会层面。

At the same time, a person's value judgments are not static. New experiences, new information, and feedback from social interaction can all change the overall structure of what a person "wants" and of how they "judge what is worth pursuing." If this structure keeps changing, we need a set of conceptual tools to explain the direction, conditions, and consequences of such change. More importantly, a person's action choices do not affect only themselves. When individuals act on their own value judgments, their behavior alters the resource conditions, mental load, and room for action of others. The study of value structure therefore cannot stop inside the individual; it must extend to the social level.

本文由此提出一个整合性问题：个体的价值结构如何形成，如何产生行动，如何在社会互动中产生外部效应，又如何被新的经验所更新？四篇分论分别处理了这一问题链的不同环节，本文将其整合为一个统一的分析框架。

This paper thus poses an integrative question: how is an individual's value structure formed, how does it generate action, how does it produce external effects in social interaction, and how is it updated by new experience? Four separate essays have previously addressed different links of this chain of questions; this paper integrates them into a unified analytical framework.

全文结构如下：第二部分讨论主体不可替代性与人生主线；第三部分定义激励函数并分析其动态性质；第四部分将激励结构引入社会层面，讨论精神负荷的外部性及其分布；第五部分分析新信息如何促成认知模型的结构性更新；第六部分总结理论链条并讨论其适用范围。

The paper is structured as follows. Part II discusses the irreplaceability of the subject and the main line of a life; Part III defines the incentive function and analyzes its dynamic properties; Part IV brings the incentive structure to the social level, discussing the externalities of mental load and their distribution; Part V analyzes how new information brings about structural updating of cognitive models; Part VI summarizes the theoretical chain and discusses its scope of application.

最后需要说明本文刻意不做的一件事。本文的核心命题是价值建构的主体不可替代性，由此产生一项写作上的自我约束：本文不提供任何具体的人生内容。如果作者把自己已经完成的价值建构写入文本，读者就可能把作者的答案当作自己的答案，绕过他们必须亲自完成的建模过程——按本文第四部分的语言，这本身就构成一种外部性。因此，本文各处的留白不是未完成，而是刻意的克制：框架只提供工具，不提供成品。

Finally, one thing this paper deliberately refrains from doing must be stated. The core proposition of this paper is the irreplaceability of the subject in value construction, and this gives rise to a self-imposed constraint on the writing: this paper provides no concrete life content. If the author were to write his own completed value construction into the text, readers might take the author's answers as their own, bypassing the modeling process they must complete in person — in the language of Part IV, this would itself constitute an externality. The blanks throughout this paper are therefore not incompleteness but deliberate restraint: the framework provides tools only, not finished products.

基于同样的理由，本文对价值结构的深层形态不作统一断言。有人的人生叙事是“浪子回头”——深层目标始终在场，只是暂时偏离；有人的人生叙事是“今日方知我是我”——是断裂与新生。两种经验都真实存在，且在第一人称视角下难以区分、也无需区分。深层结构是否恒常、如何演变，属于每个主体自行判定的范围，本文的框架对二者同时保持开放。

For the same reason, this paper makes no unified assertion about the deep morphology of value structure. Some people's life narrative is that of "the prodigal's return" — the deep goal was present all along, only temporarily departed from; others' is that of "today, at last, I know that I am myself" — a rupture and a rebirth. Both kinds of experience genuinely exist, and from the first-person perspective they are hard to distinguish — nor need they be distinguished. Whether the deep structure is constant and how it evolves fall within each subject's own judgment; the present framework remains open to both.

**研究定位与既有研究关系 / Research Positioning and Relation to Existing Research**

在正式进入分论之前，用简短篇幅说明本文在既有研究版图中的位置。

Before entering the main discussions, a brief account of this paper's position in the landscape of existing research is in order.

本文补充的是一处结构性的缝隙。关于价值与行动的研究大体分布在三个层次：规范性理论讨论人应当追求什么；描述性研究记录人们实际追求什么，以及追求过程中的心理与社会机制；元理论层面的工作则分析价值判断本身的性质与结构。三个层次各自积累了大量成果，但较少有一套框架能够同时处理四个环节——个体价值结构如何形成、如何转化为行动、如何在社会互动中产生外溢效应、又如何被新的经验所更新——并且在处理这些环节时不预设任何具体的价值内容。既有的整合尝试多半会在某一环节滑向提供规范性内容，而本框架刻意在所有环节上保持内容空白。本文的尝试正针对这一缝隙：提供一套整合性的过程框架，而不是在某一层次内部增加一条新的论证。

What this paper fills is a structural gap. Research on value and action is roughly distributed across three levels: normative theory discusses what one ought to pursue; descriptive research records what people actually pursue, together with the psychological and social mechanisms of pursuit; work at the meta-theoretical level analyzes the nature and structure of value judgment itself. Each level has accumulated substantial results. Yet few frameworks can handle four links at once — how an individual value structure is formed, how it is transformed into action, how it produces spillover effects in social interaction, and how it is updated by new experience — while presupposing no concrete value content for any of them. Existing integrative attempts mostly slide, at some link, into supplying normative content, whereas the present framework deliberately keeps the content blank at every link. This paper's attempt targets precisely this gap: to provide an integrative process framework rather than to add one more argument within a single level.

本文并非从零发明。目标设定理论关于具体目标与持续动机之关系的发现，自我决定理论关于内在动机与自主性的区分，适应负荷研究关于长期压力之累积成本的测量思路，社会熵理论关于社会系统有序程度的宏观描述，自由能原理关于认知系统最小化意外的建模方式，以及最短描述长度原则关于模型简洁性的方法论约束，都为本文提供了不同程度的启发。本文的做法是吸收上述工作中的部分洞见，将其从原有的学科语境与测量体系中剥离，进行去特征化的重新抽象，再按照“形成—行动—外溢—更新”的问题线索重新组合。因此，本文与这些理论的关系是借用与改造，而非竞争或替代；具体的借用之处已在相应章节以注释标明。

This paper is not an invention from scratch. Goal-setting theory's findings on the relation between concrete goals and sustained motivation, self-determination theory's distinction between intrinsic motivation and autonomy, the allostatic-load research tradition's approach to measuring the cumulative costs of chronic stress, social entropy theory's macro-level description of the degree of order in social systems, the free-energy principle's way of modeling cognitive systems as minimizing surprise, and the minimum-description-length principle's methodological constraint on model simplicity have all inspired this paper to varying degrees. The paper's procedure is to absorb certain insights from the work above, detach them from their original disciplinary contexts and measurement systems, re-abstract them in de-particularized form, and recombine them along the question line of "formation—action—spillover—renewal." The relation between this paper and these theories is therefore one of borrowing and transformation, not competition or replacement; the specific points of borrowing are marked in the notes of the relevant chapters.

与上述资源相比，本文框架的核心特征恰恰在于它的自我限制。第一，框架不提供任何具体的人生内容，价值目标的选择完全留给主体自身；第二，框架的规范性前提不是被断言的真理，而是需要每个主体主动勾选的世界观，不勾选则相关推论对该主体无效；第三，任何价值判断的最终确认权始终保留在第一人称经验的持有者手中，包括人工智能在内的任何外部观察者都不能接管这一位置。这三重约束使本框架区别于当前价值话语中常见的“给答案”倾向：无论答案以人生导师、成功学还是算法推荐的形式出现，其共同特征都是试图代替主体完成本应由主体亲自完成的价值建构。本文的写作原则正是对这一倾向的刻意反拨。

Compared with the resources above, the core feature of the present framework lies precisely in its self-limitation. First, the framework provides no concrete life content; the choice of value goals is left entirely to the subject. Second, the framework's normative premise is not an asserted truth but a worldview that each subject must actively "check" (as one checks a box); without that check, the relevant inferences are void for that subject. Third, the final authority to confirm any value judgment always remains with the holder of the first-person experience; no external observer, artificial intelligence included, can take over this position. These three constraints distinguish the framework from the "answer-giving" tendency common in current discourse on value: whether the answer comes in the form of a life coach, success literature, or algorithmic recommendation, the common feature is an attempt to complete, on the subject's behalf, the value construction that the subject should complete in person. The writing principle of this paper is a deliberate pushback against that tendency.

**二、主体不可替代性与人生主线 / II. The Irreplaceability of the Subject and the Main Line of a Life**

**（一）两个基本前提 / (1) Two Basic Premises**

本文首先提出两个理论前提。 第一，持续存在是获得新的第一人称经验的必要条件。这一前提并不意味着活着本身已经证明了一切人生价值，而只是指出：只要某项任务依赖主体继续获得感受、经验和反思材料，那么主体的持续存在就是其必要条件。 第二，语言存在原则上的认识论限制。即使一个主体能够非常准确地描述自己的体验，另一主体也不存在一种完全独立于第一人称经验的终极判据，用来证明后者已经获得了与前者完全相同的体验。这不等于否认沟通的有效性，而是指出第一人称体验在原则上不能被第三方彻底接管。[^2][^3]

This paper begins with two theoretical premises. First, continued existence is a necessary condition for acquiring new first-person experience. This premise does not mean that being alive has already proved all the value of a life; it only points out that as long as a task depends on the subject's continued acquisition of feelings, experiences, and material for reflection, the subject's continued existence is its necessary condition. Second, language faces a principled epistemic limit. Even if a subject can describe its own experience with great accuracy, no other subject possesses an ultimate criterion that is completely independent of first-person experience. There is therefore no way to prove that one person has obtained an experience exactly identical to another's. This does not deny the effectiveness of communication; it points out that first-person experience cannot in principle be completely taken over by a third party.

**（二）人生主线与人生支线 / (2) The Main Line and the Branch Lines of a Life**

本文定义 “人生主线” 为满足以下三个条件的任务：第一，任务以主体持续存在为必要条件；第二，任务的最终完成主体具有不可替代性；第三，任务的主体资格不可转移。第三个条件需要特别说明：主体资格不可转移不等于主体必须具备完全独立的现实行动能力。一个人可以借助语言、教育、照护或工具来完成一项任务，但只要这些外部因素最终不能取代其作为任务主体的地位，该任务仍然具有不可替代性。因此，这里的 “独立” 是主体资格意义上的独立，而非现实能力意义上的独立。

This paper defines the "main line of a life" as a task satisfying three conditions: first, the task takes the subject's continued existence as a necessary condition; second, the final completer of the task is irreplaceable; third, the subject-status of the task is non-transferable. The third condition needs special clarification: non-transferability of subject-status does not mean that the subject must possess fully independent practical ability. A person may rely on language, education, care, or tools to complete a task, but as long as these external factors ultimately cannot replace their position as the subject of the task, the task remains irreplaceable. "Independence" here is therefore independence in the sense of subject-status, not independence in the sense of practical ability.

“人生支线” 则指同样以主体持续存在为条件且具有主体不可替代性，但需要另一个具体主体即时在场的任务。主线和支线首先是任务结构上的区分，而非价值高低上的区分。

A "branch line of a life" refers to a task that likewise takes the subject's continued existence as a condition and possesses subject-irreplaceability, but requires the simultaneous presence of another concrete subject. Main lines and branch lines are, first of all, a distinction in task structure, not a distinction in value ranking.

**（三）一个规范性前提及其适用范围 / (3) A Normative Premise and Its Scope of Application**

本文引入一个独立的规范性前提：所有个体的人生主线具有相等的规范价值。需要强调的是，这一前提并非逻辑必然事实，也不是一条经验性规律，而是一种价值选择。它只有在主体主动接受时才具有规范效力——可以把这种接受理解为一次主动的“勾选”：不勾选，本文的规范性推论对该主体无效；勾选之后，它们才构成约束。因此，本文后续关于人与人之间互动的讨论，也只在认同这一前提的主体之间才构成行为指导；对于不认同这一前提的主体，本文不宣称其具有普遍约束力。

This paper introduces an independent normative premise: the main lines of all individuals' lives have equal normative value. It must be emphasized that this premise is not a logically necessary fact, nor an empirical law, but a value choice. It has normative force only when a subject actively accepts it — this acceptance can be understood as an active "check": without the check, this paper's normative inferences are void for that subject; once checked, they become binding. The later discussions of interaction between persons therefore constitute behavioral guidance only among subjects who accept this premise; for subjects who do not accept it, this paper claims no universal binding force.

在此基础上，如果救人行为恢复了另一主体继续其自身主线的现实机会，且我们认可主线延续具有规范价值，那么救人行为就具有相应的工具性价值。救人要求行动者和被救者同时存在；同时，在死亡威胁足够紧迫的情形下，有效的行动窗口内通常不存在另一个可替代的行动者，当前拯救者因而满足支线所要求的不可替代性。救人由此属于支线结构。需要坦白的是：这种不可替代性既不可证实，也不可证伪——事件唯一、过去不可复现、没有对照可能，它属于人类认知边界内的不可判定命题。而整段规范推论也并非逻辑必然结论，而是建立在明确价值选择之上的。

On this basis, if an act of rescue restores another subject's realistic opportunity to continue their own main line, and if we acknowledge that the continuation of a main line has normative value, then the act of rescue has corresponding instrumental value. Rescue requires the simultaneous presence of rescuer and rescued; at the same time, when the threat of death is sufficiently urgent, there is usually no other substitutable actor within the effective window of action, and the rescuer at hand thus satisfies the irreplaceability required of a branch line. Rescue therefore belongs to the branch-line structure. It must be frankly admitted that this irreplaceability can be neither verified nor falsified — the event is unique, the past cannot be replayed, and no control is possible; it is an undecidable proposition within the boundaries of human cognition. Nor is the whole normative inference a logically necessary conclusion; it is built upon an explicit value choice.

**（四）自我认识作为主体不可替代的任务 / (4) Self-Knowledge as a Task Only the Subject Can Complete**

本文所说的自我认识，不是人格标签的罗列，也不是对性格特征的静态描述。它是一种持续进行的主体自我建模过程：个体以感受、行为、欲望、选择以及历史经验作为材料，试图找出这些材料背后较稳定的价值结构。这一过程最终涉及两个核心问题：我真正追求什么，以及我依据什么标准判断某种状态或行动对我而言具有更高或更低的价值。

What this paper means by self-knowledge is not a list of personality labels, nor a static description of character traits. It is an ongoing process of self-modeling by the subject: the individual takes feelings, actions, desires, choices, and historical experience as material, and tries to find the relatively stable value structure behind this material. The process ultimately involves two core questions: what do I truly pursue, and by what standard do I judge that a state or an action has higher or lower value for me.

自我认识首先满足生命依赖性 —— 只要这一过程仍然需要新的经验材料，主体持续存在就是必要条件。其次，自我认识具有不可转移的主体性：其他人可以提供关于主体的观察，可以帮助整理经验，也可以提出解释，但最终需要判断的是这些经验对主体自身意味着什么、如何进入主体自己的价值结构。需要说明的是，这一不可替代性的根据并非“他人无法完全理解主体”这一认识论事实本身，而是最终确认与修正的主体资格不可转移。因此，他人可以参与一个人的自我认识，却不能替代这个主体成为其自身价值结构的最终确认者和修正者。

Self-knowledge first satisfies life-dependence — as long as the process still requires new experiential material, the subject's continued existence is a necessary condition. Second, self-knowledge has non-transferable subjectivity: others can provide observations about the subject, can help organize experience, and can propose interpretations, but what ultimately must be judged is what these experiences mean for the subject and how they enter the subject's own value structure. It should be noted that the ground of this irreplaceability is not the epistemic fact itself that "others cannot fully understand the subject," but the non-transferability of the subject-status of final confirmation and revision. Others can therefore participate in a person's self-knowledge, but cannot replace the subject as the final confirmer and reviser of its own value structure.

**三、激励函数：价值结构的动态模型 / III. The Incentive Function: A Dynamic Model of Value Structure**

**（一）激励函数的定义 / (1) Defining the Incentive Function**

本文以 “激励函数” 指称主体对可能状态进行价值赋值并形成行动倾向的整体结构。这里的 “函数” 是理论意义上的抽象，而非一个需要预设为真实存在于大脑中的数学公式。它表示的是：从情境和可能状态出发，主体如何进行价值赋值，以及这种赋值如何生成行动倾向。

This paper uses "incentive function" to name the overall structure by which a subject assigns value to possible states and forms tendencies to act. "Function" here is a theoretical abstraction, not a mathematical formula that must be presupposed to really exist in the brain. What it expresses is: starting from situations and possible states, how the subject assigns value, and how this assignment generates tendencies to act.

激励函数是否恒常、如何演变，因人而异，由主体自行判定。主体不断获得新的经验，也不断对已有经验进行重新解释，因此主体的自我模型更接近一个动态更新的结构。过去经验可以影响当前偏好，但不能自动规定当前选择；当前选择又会形成新的经验，并进一步影响未来的价值结构。于是，自我认识不是先得到一个完整模型再依据模型生活，而是在生活过程中不断修正模型。

Whether the incentive function is constant, and how it evolves, varies from person to person and is for each subject to judge. The subject constantly acquires new experience and constantly reinterprets existing experience; the subject's self-model is therefore closer to a dynamically updating structure. Past experience can influence current preferences but cannot automatically determine current choices; current choices in turn form new experience and further influence the future value structure. Self-knowledge is thus not a matter of first obtaining a complete model and then living according to it, but of continually revising the model in the course of living.

**（二）欲望、目标状态与期盼结构 / (2) Desire, Goal States, and the Structure of Aspiration**

日常语言往往把欲望表达为一个对象，例如财富、职位或成功，但对象与目标状态并不总是相同。一个人追求财富，可能因为财富被其视为安全、自由或控制感的条件；另一个人希望某个真相被发现，可能并不是因为真相能够直接提高自己的舒适程度，而是因为 “知道真相” 本身就是其所追求的状态。因此，本文将欲望定义为：主体对某种可能状态所作出的正向价值赋值。这一状态既可以属于主体自身，也可以属于主体之外的外部世界。外部对象通常是主体认为能够促成该状态的条件，但并非所有欲望都需要通过自身心理状态来定义。这一界定使本文能够避免将全部欲望还原为心理享乐主义。

Everyday language often expresses desire as an object — wealth, a position, or success, for example — but the object and the goal state are not always the same. A person who pursues wealth may do so because wealth is seen as a condition of security, freedom, or a sense of control. Another person may hope that a certain truth is discovered — not because the truth would directly increase their comfort, but because "knowing the truth" is itself the state they pursue. This paper therefore defines desire as the subject's positive value assignment to some possible state. That state may belong to the subject itself or to the external world beyond the subject. External objects are usually conditions the subject believes can bring about that state, but not all desires need to be defined through one's own psychological states. This definition allows the paper to avoid reducing all desire to psychological hedonism.

“期盼行为” 是主体为了促成某一未来状态而实施的行动，“期盼结果” 则是主体试图实现的未来状态。二者存在因果联系，但不具有结构上的同一性。一个人通过学习获得资格 —— 学习属于期盼行为，资格属于期盼结果。当资格已经获得之后，学习这一行为原本承担的工具性作用可能下降。因此，目标实现之后动力变化并不意味着此前目标无意义，也不意味着动力衰减必然是消极现象。它只是说明：实现结果的行动机制，不必然包含在结果自身之中。

An "aspirational action" is an action the subject performs in order to bring about some future state; an "aspirational outcome" is the future state the subject tries to realize. The two are causally connected but not structurally identical. A person acquires a qualification through study — study is the aspirational action, the qualification the aspirational outcome. Once the qualification has been obtained, the instrumental role originally carried by the action of studying may decline. A change of motivation after a goal is realized therefore does not mean that the previous goal was meaningless, nor that a decline of motivation is necessarily a negative phenomenon. It only shows that the action mechanism that realizes an outcome is not necessarily contained within the outcome itself.

**（三）目标自我包含性 / (3) Goal Self-Containment**

如果一个人的目标结构主要依赖 “最终获得某个结果”，那么结果实现以后，原有行为的必要性就可能下降。这会产生一个常见动态：目标形成、持续追求、目标实现、原有动力下降、重新形成目标。这一过程本身并不必然需要被评价为问题 —— 一种生活方式完全可以由一系列不断完成的目标构成。

If a person's goal structure depends mainly on "finally obtaining some result," then once the result is realized, the necessity of the original behavior may decline. This produces a common dynamic: a goal is formed, pursued over time, realized; the original motivation declines; a new goal is formed. This process itself does not necessarily have to be evaluated as a problem — a way of life can perfectly well consist of a series of continually completed goals.

本文真正关心的是另一种情况：主体试图建立长期而连续的价值结构，却不断把最终结果误认为价值的全部，于是每次目标实现都造成原有动力结构的瓦解。为此，本文提出 “目标自我包含性”：一个持续性的目标，其定义不仅包括期望结果，也包括使这一结果能够持续存在的行动方式。例如，如果一个人把健康视为长期目标，那么健康并不只是某一次检测中的指标，也包括长期维持健康状态的生活方式。因此，一个稳定目标的结果结构应当在一定程度上包含产生和维持该结果的行为。

What this paper is really concerned with is another situation: the subject tries to build a long-term, continuous value structure, yet repeatedly mistakes the final result for the whole of value, so that each realization of a goal causes the collapse of the original motivational structure. To address this, the paper proposes "goal self-containment": for an enduring goal, its definition includes not only the desired result but also the mode of action that keeps the result in existence. For example, if a person regards health as a long-term goal, then health is not merely the readings of one particular examination, but also the way of life that maintains the state of health over the long term. The result-structure of a stable goal should therefore contain, to a certain degree, the behavior that produces and maintains that result.

这一命题并非说所有人生目标都必须稳定，更不是说目标替换必然有害。它只是指出：如果主体追求的是一种连续的价值结构，那么目标若完全依赖一次性结果，便容易在结果实现后失去自身的行动基础。[^4][^5]

This proposition does not say that all life goals must be stable, still less that replacing goals is necessarily harmful. It only points out that if what the subject pursues is a continuous value structure, then a goal that depends entirely on a one-off result easily loses its own basis for action once the result is realized.

**（四）预设与直觉 / (4) Presupposition and Intuition**

现实判断总是在信息不完整的条件下进行。如果主体必须等到所有信息都获得之后才能行动，那么大量实际决策无法发生。因此，主体需要预设。本文将预设定义为：在信息不完整条件下，用于暂时缩小解释范围、形成行动方向和降低认知成本的假设。预设的价值来自效率，预设的风险也来自效率 —— 一个暂时成立的假设，如果被错误地提升为不可修改的真理，就可能迫使新的经验不断被解释为原有体系的例外。

Real-world judgment is always made under conditions of incomplete information. If the subject had to wait until all information was obtained before acting, a great many actual decisions could never occur. The subject therefore needs presuppositions. This paper defines a presupposition as a hypothesis used, under conditions of incomplete information, to temporarily narrow the range of interpretation, form a direction for action, and reduce cognitive costs. The value of presuppositions comes from efficiency, and their risk comes from efficiency as well — a provisionally valid hypothesis, if mistakenly promoted to an unmodifiable truth, may force new experience to be interpreted again and again as an exception to the original system.

预设之所以合理，并不是因为它永远正确，而是因为它可以在新的证据出现后被修改。当新经验与当前模型产生矛盾时，主体可以先修正局部解释；如果局部修正越来越多且系统内部出现更深层次的冲突，那么更合理的做法是重新审查原有预设。因此，认知上的成熟并不是拥有一个永远不变的初始判断，而是具有足够低的 “预设粘性”。

A presupposition is reasonable not because it is forever correct, but because it can be revised when new evidence appears. When new experience conflicts with the current model, the subject can first revise local interpretations; if local revisions keep accumulating and deeper conflicts appear within the system, the more reasonable course is to re-examine the original presuppositions. Cognitive maturity therefore does not consist in possessing an initial judgment that never changes, but in having sufficiently low "presupposition stickiness."

直觉可以被理解为一种快速生成预设的机制。长期经验会在主体内部形成大量压缩后的模式，因此人在许多情况下不需要重新从头推理就可以迅速形成判断。这种能力可以降低认知成本，但直觉的效率并不等于绝对正确。熟悉环境中的高效判断，在结构发生改变的环境中可能失效。因此，直觉最适合被理解为高效率的初始模型，而非终极真实性来源。

Intuition can be understood as a mechanism for rapidly generating presuppositions. Long-term experience forms, inside the subject, a large number of compressed patterns, so that in many situations a person can quickly form a judgment without reasoning from scratch. This ability can reduce cognitive costs, but the efficiency of intuition does not amount to absolute correctness. Efficient judgment in a familiar environment may fail in an environment whose structure has changed. Intuition is therefore best understood as a high-efficiency initial model, not as an ultimate source of truth.

**四、社会层面：精神负荷的外部性与分布 / IV. The Social Level: Externalities and Distribution of Mental Load**

**（一）分析变量的界定 / (1) Defining the Analytical Variable**

本文引入一种社会层面的分析变量，用于描述个体与群体在物质条件、精神负荷以及二者之间转换关系的状态。需要明确的是，这不是热力学意义上的熵，也不是一种已经能够独立测量的自然实体，而是一种人为构造的理论变量。其合理性取决于能否用统一的概念结构解释若干彼此相关的社会现象。

This paper introduces a social-level analytical variable to describe the states of individuals and groups with respect to material conditions, mental load, and the conversion relation between the two. It should be made clear that this is not entropy in the thermodynamic sense, nor a natural entity that can already be measured independently, but an artificially constructed theoretical variable. Its reasonableness depends on whether a unified conceptual structure can explain several interrelated social phenomena.

这一名称此前已在其他理论中出现过[^6]，因此本文不主张名称的原创性，而是强调不同的理论焦点：本文主要关注个体精神调节负荷及其社会外部性，以及物质状态与精神状态之间的相互作用。与此同时，关于社会失范的研究以及关于适应负荷的应激研究，为本文的分析提供了两个理论邻近对象。[^7][^8]

This name has appeared before in other theories, so the paper claims no originality for the name and instead emphasizes a different theoretical focus: this paper is mainly concerned with individual mental regulatory load and its social externalities, and with the interaction between material states and mental states. At the same time, research on social anomie and stress research on allostatic load provide two theoretically neighboring objects for the present analysis.

**（二）精神调节负荷 / (2) Mental Regulatory Load**

本文不再把焦虑、不安或认知混乱直接视为同一个变量。一个人可以在情绪上非常焦虑，但在认知上拥有高度结构化的世界观；也可以拥有高度复杂的思维结构，却保持相对平静。因此，本文将核心概念定义为：主体为了维持自身目标状态、处理环境不确定性以及调节社会关系而承担的整体精神调节负荷。焦虑、持续冲突和不确定性可以成为这种负荷的来源，但它们本身并不是负荷的同义词。

This paper no longer treats anxiety, unease, or cognitive confusion as directly one and the same variable. A person can be emotionally very anxious while possessing a highly structured worldview at the cognitive level; one can also possess a highly complex structure of thought while remaining relatively calm. The core concept of this paper is therefore defined as the overall mental regulatory load that a subject bears in order to maintain its own goal states, handle environmental uncertainty, and regulate social relations. Anxiety, persistent conflict, and uncertainty can be sources of this load, but they are not themselves synonyms of the load.

这一概念与应激研究中的 “适应负荷” 存在亲缘关系，但二者仍然不同：适应负荷具有明确的生理研究传统，而本文的分析变量目前仍是哲学和社会理论中的分析工具。[^9]

This concept is related to "allostatic load" in stress research, but the two are still different: allostatic load has a well-defined physiological research tradition, whereas the analytical variable of this paper remains, for now, an analytical tool in philosophy and social theory.

**（三）物质条件与精神负荷的关系 / (3) The Relation Between Material Conditions and Mental Load**

本文提出一个经验性的弱假设：物质条件能够在一定程度上影响精神状态，但这种影响并非简单线性关系。当主体处于严重资源匮乏状态时，基本物质改善可能带来非常明显的精神收益；当基本需要逐渐得到满足之后，继续增加相同资源所带来的边际收益可能降低。[^10]物质条件并不直接决定精神状态 —— 同样的收入、住房或社会资源可能对不同主体产生不同影响。因此，本文引入 “物质到精神的转换” 这一概念，用于描述一个主体如何把外部资源转化为自身所追求的稳定、满足或安全状态。

This paper proposes a weak empirical hypothesis: material conditions can influence mental states to a certain degree, but the influence is not a simple linear relation. When a subject is in a state of severe resource scarcity, basic material improvement may bring very significant mental gains; once basic needs are gradually met, the marginal gain from continuing to add the same resources may decline. Material conditions do not directly determine mental states — the same income, housing, or social resources may have different effects on different subjects. This paper therefore introduces the concept of "material-to-mental conversion" to describe how a subject transforms external resources into the stability, satisfaction, or security it pursues.

但这里存在严格的理论限制：如果 “转换能力” 可以无条件地解释一切异常，那么理论将失去可检验性。因此，转换能力目前只能作为理论变量，不能用作万能解释。

However, there is a strict theoretical limit here: if "conversion capacity" could unconditionally explain every anomaly, the theory would lose testability. Conversion capacity can therefore serve only as a theoretical variable for now, not as an all-purpose explanation.

个体的精神状态并非只能增加、不能恢复。主体具有适应、习惯化、重新解释和恢复的能力。因此，更合理的命题是：在持续刺激、长期不确定性或者缺乏有效调节机制的条件下，精神调节负荷可能发生累积；与此同时，主体和环境中的恢复机制也可能降低这一负荷。这一模型允许两种方向同时存在，因而是动态的而非必然单向增长的概念。

An individual's mental state is not something that can only increase and never recover. Subjects have the capacities of adaptation, habituation, reinterpretation, and recovery. A more reasonable proposition is therefore: under conditions of persistent stimulation, long-term uncertainty, or the absence of effective regulatory mechanisms, mental regulatory load may accumulate; at the same time, recovery mechanisms in the subject and the environment may also reduce this load. This model allows both directions to exist at once, and is thus a dynamic concept rather than one of necessarily one-way growth.

**（四）状态的外部性及其社会分布 / (4) Externalities of States and Their Social Distribution**

个体并非封闭系统。言语、行为、资源配置、组织制度和人际关系，都可能改变其他主体的状态。因此，一个人的激励结构不仅决定自身行动，也可能对周围人产生外部性。例如，一个组织中的长期不确定性可能由一个人制造，却由整个团队共同承担；某种资源配置方式可能降低一个主体的精神负荷，却把风险转移给另一个主体。社会系统并不仅仅由个体状态组成，还由状态在主体之间的转移组成。

Individuals are not closed systems. Speech, behavior, resource allocation, organizational institutions, and interpersonal relations can all change the states of other subjects. A person's incentive structure therefore not only determines their own actions but may also produce externalities for those around them. For example, long-term uncertainty in an organization may be created by one person yet borne by the whole team; a certain way of allocating resources may reduce one subject's mental load while transferring risk to another. A social system consists not only of individual states, but also of the transfer of states between subjects.

权力在这一框架中具有两个主要作用方向：话语权扩大信息、解释框架和情绪状态的传播范围；实际权力改变资源分配、行为约束和制度条件。但二者并非互斥 —— 话语权完全可能改变物质配置，实际权力也完全可能产生巨大的精神压力。

Power has two main directions of action within this framework: discursive power enlarges the reach of information, interpretive frames, and emotional states; material power changes resource distribution, behavioral constraints, and institutional conditions. But the two are not mutually exclusive — discursive power may well change material allocation, and material power may well generate enormous mental pressure.

道德可以在一定程度上被理解为社会对于行为外部性的规范机制。如果一种行为持续对他人造成不必要的心理负担，那么社会可能逐渐形成针对这种行为的规范评价。但不能因此推出所有令他人感到不快的行为都构成道德问题 —— 精神状态具有高度主观性。社会理论真正需要关注的是可重复、结构化并具有稳定外部影响的行为模式。

Morality can be understood, to a certain degree, as society's normative mechanism for the externalities of behavior. If a kind of behavior persistently imposes unnecessary psychological burdens on others, society may gradually form normative evaluations targeting it. But it does not follow that all behavior that displeases others constitutes a moral problem — mental states are highly subjective. What social theory really needs to attend to are patterns of behavior that are repeatable, structured, and have stable external effects.

法律的优势在于能够处理边界较为清晰、证据较易形成的行为侵害，如财产损失、人身伤害和明确的契约违约。精神层面的损害则复杂得多 —— 霸凌、操纵、羞辱和长期关系控制可能产生现实后果，但其责任边界和因果关系通常更难确定。因此，法律对精神领域覆盖有限，可以被部分理解为测量、举证与判定成本的结果，而不必直接归因于制度对精神问题的冷漠。

The advantage of law lies in its ability to handle infringements whose boundaries are relatively clear and whose evidence is relatively easy to form, such as property loss, personal injury, and explicit breach of contract. Mental harm is far more complex — bullying, manipulation, humiliation, and long-term relational control can produce real consequences, but their boundaries of responsibility and causal relations are usually harder to determine. The law's limited coverage of the mental domain can therefore be understood in part as a result of the costs of measurement, proof, and adjudication, and need not be directly attributed to institutional indifference to mental problems.

传统阶层理论主要关注财富、收入、职业和制度权力。本文尝试增加一个补充维度：不同群体承担状态负荷的能力是否存在结构性差异。一个群体可能长期承担较高的资源风险和精神调节成本，而另一个群体拥有更多缓冲机制。如果这种关系长期存在，那么阶层差异不仅表现为财富差距，也表现为状态成本与状态缓冲能力的不对称。

Traditional class theory focuses mainly on wealth, income, occupation, and institutional power. This paper attempts to add a supplementary dimension: whether there are structural differences in different groups' capacity to bear state load. One group may long bear higher resource risk and mental regulatory costs, while another group possesses more buffering mechanisms. If such a relation persists over the long term, then class differences are manifested not only as wealth gaps, but also as asymmetries in state costs and state-buffering capacity.

**（五）物质与精神的相对脱钩 / (5) The Relative Decoupling of Material Conditions and Mental States**

如果主体把全部精神稳定性建立在外部物质条件上，那么资源变化就可能直接转化为精神状态变化。相反，如果主体已经建立了一部分相对独立于外部资源的价值结构，那么物质条件变化对整体精神状态的影响可能减弱。本文把这种关系称为 “物质与精神的相对脱钩”—— 这里的 “相对” 十分重要。这并不意味着主体不再受到贫困、疾病或资源约束影响，它只意味着外部资源不再成为主体精神价值的唯一决定变量。这一命题连接了个体价值结构与社会分析：个体如何构建自身价值结构，会改变其对外部资源变化的敏感度。

If a subject builds all of its mental stability on external material conditions, then changes in resources may be directly converted into changes in mental state. Conversely, if the subject has already built a part of its value structure relatively independent of external resources, the influence of changes in material conditions on the overall mental state may weaken. This paper calls this relation the "relative decoupling of material conditions and mental states" — the word "relative" is very important here. It does not mean that the subject is no longer affected by poverty, disease, or resource constraints; it only means that external resources no longer serve as the sole determining variable of the subject's mental value. This proposition connects individual value structure with social analysis: how an individual constructs its own value structure changes its sensitivity to changes in external resources.

**五、认知更新：信息如何改变价值结构 / V. Cognitive Renewal: How Information Changes Value Structure**

**（一）输入规模与影响规模的非对称性 / (1) The Asymmetry Between Input Scale and Impact Scale**

复杂认知系统中的输入并不一定按照输入规模产生等比例结果。其机制链条可以明确为：信息改变认知模型，认知模型改变激励结构，激励结构改变行为。一段很短的信息，如果改变了主体理解过去经验的方式，即作用于链条的第一环，那么它可能沿着链条进一步改变主体之后的判断、选择和行为。因此，一个信息输入最终产生多大影响，取决于它作用于链条的哪一环，而不能只根据输入本身的规模判断。同时，链条的每一环都受简洁性约束：能够用更少环节解释的变化，不应引入更多环节。本文将此称为一种关于认知模型变化的描述：原本分散的经验开始获得更高程度的组织。需要强调的是，这并非一种新的物理学概念，而是对认知结构变化的比喻性说明。[^11]

Input into a complex cognitive system does not necessarily produce results proportional to the scale of the input. The mechanism chain can be made explicit: information changes the cognitive model, the cognitive model changes the incentive structure, and the incentive structure changes behavior. A very short piece of information, if it changes the way the subject understands past experience — that is, if it acts on the first link of the chain — may then travel along the chain and further change the subject's later judgments, choices, and actions. How much impact an informational input ultimately has therefore depends on which link of the chain it acts upon; it cannot be judged by the scale of the input alone. At the same time, every link of the chain is subject to a simplicity constraint: a change that can be explained with fewer links should not be explained by introducing more. This paper calls this a description of change in cognitive models: originally scattered experience begins to acquire a higher degree of organization. It should be emphasized that this is not a new concept in physics, but a metaphorical account of change in cognitive structure.

**（二）模型选择的标准 / (2) Criteria for Model Selection**

一个能够 “解释一切” 的模型可能只是无限增加特殊规则。因此，模型选择还需要考虑一致性、新经验适应能力以及简洁性。一个好的自我模型至少满足几个条件：能够覆盖较多经验；在不同经验之间保持内部一致；面对新的经验时具有修正能力；不需要无限增加独立规则。本文借鉴最短描述长度原则来讨论模型复杂度 —— 即将模型和数据的描述长度结合起来考察模型选择 —— 但本文的分析仍处于哲学层面，而非信息论模型本身。[^12]

A model that can "explain everything" may merely be adding special rules without limit. Model selection therefore also needs to consider consistency, the capacity to adapt to new experience, and simplicity. A good self-model satisfies at least several conditions: it can cover a relatively large range of experience; it remains internally consistent across different experiences; it has the capacity to revise itself in the face of new experience; and it does not need to add independent rules without limit. This paper borrows the minimum-description-length principle to discuss model complexity — that is, examining model selection by combining the description lengths of model and data — but the present analysis remains at the philosophical level rather than being an information-theoretic model itself.

一个新经验如果不能被当前模型解释，有两种基本处理方式：增加特殊规则，或者重新审查模型。特殊规则并不必然是错误的，因为现实本身存在真正的特殊情况。但如果模型越来越依赖特殊规则，那么模型复杂度就会迅速增加。因此，异常经验最重要的理论意义并不在于证明旧模型错误，而在于指出旧模型可能已经到达解释能力的边界 —— 异常可以成为理论更新的入口。

When a new experience cannot be explained by the current model, there are two basic ways of handling it: add special rules, or re-examine the model. Special rules are not necessarily wrong, because reality itself contains genuine special cases. But if a model relies more and more on special rules, its complexity increases rapidly. The most important theoretical significance of anomalous experience therefore lies not in proving the old model wrong, but in indicating that the old model may have reached the boundary of its explanatory capacity — anomalies can become the entry point of theoretical renewal.

**（三）人工智能作为辅助建模工具 / (3) Artificial Intelligence as an Auxiliary Modeling Tool**

人工智能可以作为自我认识过程中的辅助工具。主体可以将自己的感受、行为、选择和历史经验转译成语言材料，由人工智能帮助整理和比较。但人工智能并不能因此成为主体 “真正的自我” 的外部权威 —— 它不拥有主体全部的第一人称经验。它能够做的是发现材料之间的潜在关联、提出候选解释、发现模型内部的冲突，并根据新的材料更新候选模型。值得注意的是，主体自身在事前同样不知道答案——这恰恰说明人工智能为何可以成为共同建模者：双方都只能在既有材料上工作；也同样说明它为何不能成为最终裁判者：解释的最终确认权不在算力更强的一方，而在唯一拥有全部第一人称材料的一方。因此，人工智能最合理的角色是共同建模者，而非最终裁判者。这一定位也与前文关于主体不可替代性的论证保持一致。

Artificial intelligence can serve as an auxiliary tool in the process of self-knowledge. The subject can translate its feelings, actions, choices, and historical experience into linguistic material, and let AI help organize and compare it. But AI cannot thereby become an external authority on the subject's "true self" — it does not possess the whole of the subject's first-person experience. What it can do is discover potential connections among the materials, propose candidate interpretations, detect conflicts within the model, and update candidate models in light of new material. It is worth noting that the subject itself does not know the answer in advance either. This is precisely why AI can become a co-modeler: both sides can only work on the existing material. However, it is also why AI cannot become the final arbiter — the final authority to confirm an interpretation lies not with the side that has more computing power, but with the only side that possesses all the first-person material. The most reasonable role for AI is therefore that of co-modeler, not final arbiter. This positioning is also consistent with the earlier argument about the irreplaceability of the subject.

如果人工智能提出一个关于主体的解释，不能因为语言听起来有说服力就接受它。至少需要考虑覆盖性（解释能否覆盖更多既有经验）、一致性（解释是否与其他经验产生不可解决的冲突）、预测能力（面对新经验时能否比竞争模型更准确地预期主体的判断或行动倾向）以及简洁性（是否需要大量特殊规则才能保持表面上的解释能力）。

If an AI proposes an interpretation of the subject, it should not be accepted merely because the language sounds persuasive. At least the following need to be considered: coverage (whether the interpretation covers more of the existing experience), consistency (whether the interpretation generates irresolvable conflicts with other experience), predictive power (whether, in the face of new experience, it can anticipate the subject's judgments or action tendencies more accurately than competing models), and simplicity (whether large numbers of special rules are needed to maintain apparent explanatory power).

**（四）历史经验：不可逆性与重构 / (4) Historical Experience: Irreversibility and Reconstruction**

历史经验中存在一类问题，即已经发生的损失无法通过新的损失被撤销。如果一个人已经死亡，那么对加害者实施新的伤害并不能归还死者已经失去的生命时间。因此，本文认为复仇不能实现严格意义上的 “历史平账”。但这并不意味着复仇只有心理功能 —— 它也可能具有惩罚、威慑、社会规范确认、身份和荣誉维护等社会功能。本文只否定一种特定命题：新的损失不能真正使过去已经发生的损失消失。

Among historical experiences there is a class of problems in which a loss that has already occurred cannot be undone by a new loss. If a person has died, inflicting new harm on the wrongdoer cannot return the life-time the dead person has already lost. This paper therefore holds that revenge cannot achieve a "settling of historical accounts" in the strict sense. But this does not mean that revenge has only psychological functions — it may also have social functions such as punishment, deterrence, confirmation of social norms, and the maintenance of identity and honor. This paper denies only one specific proposition: a new loss cannot truly make a past loss disappear.

与无法逆转的历史损失不同，个体还会继承大量过去形成的条件 —— 语言、技术、制度、教育、他人的劳动以及历史形成的社会资源，都进入当前行动的可能空间。因此，个体不可能从完全没有前提的状态开始行动。但历史条件与道德命令需要区别：继承了一项条件，不等于自动形成无限的道德债务。某些具体情形确实需要讨论责任，例如明确的不义所得、直接的权利剥夺或者尚未解决的制度性伤害。因此，“继承” 并不是对所有历史遗产的道德正当化，而只是描述一个事实：当前主体总是在既有因果条件中行动。

Unlike irreversible historical losses, individuals also inherit a large number of conditions formed in the past — language, technology, institutions, education, the labor of others, and historically formed social resources all enter the space of possibility for current action. No individual can therefore act from a state entirely free of premises. But historical conditions and moral commands need to be distinguished: inheriting a condition does not automatically generate an unlimited moral debt. Certain concrete situations do require discussion of responsibility — for example, clear unjust gains, direct deprivation of rights, or unresolved institutional harm. "Inheritance" is therefore not a moral justification of all historical legacies, but only the description of a fact: the acting subject always acts within existing causal conditions.

历史可以解释当前主体如何形成，却不能自动规定其未来行动。一个人的过去经历可以成为恐惧来源，也可以成为判断力形成的条件 —— 同一段经历甚至可能在不同人生阶段具有不同意义。所谓价值重构，并不是对事实进行修改，而是重新确定历史事实在当前激励函数中的位置。过去提供材料，当前主体决定这些材料如何进入未来行动。

History can explain how the current subject was formed, but it cannot automatically prescribe the subject's future action. A person's past experience can become a source of fear, or a condition for the formation of judgment — the same stretch of experience may even carry different meanings at different stages of a life. So-called value reconstruction is not a revision of facts, but a re-determination of the position of historical facts within the current incentive function. The past provides the material; the present subject decides how this material enters future action.

**六、综合讨论：一条理论链条 / VI. General Discussion: A Theoretical Chain**

至此，本文可以形成一条连续的理论线索： 主体不可替代性构成分析的起点：自我认识是一种主体资格不可转移的持续性任务，其对象是主体自身的价值结构。这一任务之所以不可替代，并非因为某种神秘本质，而是因为第一人称经验在原则上不能被第三方完全接管。

At this point the paper can form a continuous theoretical line. The irreplaceability of the subject constitutes the starting point of the analysis: self-knowledge is a continuing task whose subject-status is non-transferable, and whose object is the subject's own value structure. This task is irreplaceable not because of any mysterious essence, but because first-person experience cannot in principle be completely taken over by a third party.

激励函数描述这一价值结构的动态机制：主体通过欲望、目标、预设和直觉，对可能状态进行价值赋值并形成行动倾向。主体对激励函数的认识不断被新经验修正，而目标自我包含性决定了长期价值结构能否在结果实现后继续维持自身。

The incentive function describes the dynamic mechanism of this value structure: through desire, goals, presuppositions, and intuition, the subject assigns value to possible states and forms tendencies to act. The subject's knowledge of the incentive function is continually revised by new experience, and goal self-containment determines whether a long-term value structure can continue to sustain itself after results are realized.

社会层面的精神负荷分布说明个体行为的溢出效应：当不同主体依照各自激励结构行动时，其行为会改变他人的资源条件与精神调节负荷。这种外部性在权力、道德、法律和阶层结构中呈现系统性的不对称分布。物质与精神可以在一定程度上相对脱钩，其程度取决于个体价值结构的独立程度。

The social distribution of mental load accounts for the spillover effects of individual behavior: when different subjects act according to their respective incentive structures, their behavior changes the resource conditions and mental regulatory load of others. Such externalities display systematically asymmetric distribution across power, morality, law, and class structure. Material conditions and mental states can be relatively decoupled to a certain degree, the degree depending on how independent the individual's value structure is.

认知模型更新解释了变化如何发生：较小的信息输入如果能够改变主体组织既有经验的模型，就可能进一步改变未来的价值判断和行动倾向。模型选择需要在覆盖范围、一致性、预测能力和简洁性之间取得平衡，而历史经验虽然不可逆，但其在激励函数中的位置可以被重新安排。

The updating of cognitive models explains how change occurs: a relatively small informational input, if it can change the model by which the subject organizes existing experience, may further change future value judgments and action tendencies. Model selection needs to balance coverage, consistency, predictive power, and simplicity; and although historical experience is irreversible, its position within the incentive function can be rearranged.

这四部分并非四个独立主题，而可以被统一描述为：主体如何形成价值结构，价值结构如何产生行动，行动如何进入社会并产生外部效应，新的信息如何反过来改变价值结构。

These four parts are not four independent topics; they can be described in a unified way: how the subject forms a value structure, how the value structure generates action, how action enters society and produces external effects, and how new information in turn changes the value structure.

需要再次强调的是，本文关于 “所有个体的人生主线具有相等的规范价值” 这一前提是一种主动的价值选择，而非逻辑必然事实。因此，本文的全部规范性推论 —— 尤其是关于人与人之间如何互动的部分 —— 只对认同这一前提的主体具有指导效力。对于不认同这一前提的主体，本文提供的是描述性分析框架，而非规范性命令。

It must be emphasized once again that this paper's premise — "the main lines of all individuals' lives have equal normative value" — is an active value choice, not a logically necessary fact. All the normative inferences of this paper, especially those concerning how persons should interact, therefore have guiding force only for subjects who accept this premise. For subjects who do not accept it, what this paper provides is a descriptive analytical framework, not a normative command.

**七、结论 / VII. Conclusion**

本文提出了一个整合性分析框架，用以连接个体价值形成、行动选择、社会外部效应和认知更新四个层面。其主要概念 —— 主体不可替代性、激励函数、精神调节负荷与认知模型更新 —— 彼此之间相互衔接。

This paper has proposed an integrative analytical framework connecting four levels: individual value formation, action choice, social external effects, and cognitive renewal. Its main concepts — the irreplaceability of the subject, the incentive function, mental regulatory load, and the updating of cognitive models — interlock with one another.

这一框架并不试图证明某种预先存在的人生目的，也不主张自我认识在经验意义上一定比其他人生内容更重要。它所做的是提供一套概念工具，用以分析 “我想要什么”“我如何行动”“我的行动如何影响他人” 以及 “我如何被新的经验改变” 这四个相互关联的问题。

The framework does not attempt to prove any pre-existing purpose of life, nor does it claim that self-knowledge is necessarily more important, in empirical terms, than other life content. What it does is provide a set of conceptual tools for analyzing four interrelated questions: "What do I want?" "How do I act?" "How do my actions affect others?" and "How am I changed by new experience?"

本框架目前仍处于理论构建阶段。未来的研究需要分别从认知科学、社会研究和信息论三个方向对其核心变量进行进一步的形式化与经验检验，包括精神调节负荷的观察方式、物质到精神转换的独立测量、个体状态向群体变量的聚合方式，以及社会状态转移的识别方法。这些问题的解决不是理论定义本身能够完成的，但本文希望提供一个可供后续讨论和检验的起点。

The framework is currently still at the stage of theoretical construction. Future research will need to further formalize and empirically test its core variables from three directions — cognitive science, social research, and information theory — including ways of observing mental regulatory load, independent measurement of material-to-mental conversion, ways of aggregating individual states into group-level variables, and methods for identifying the transfer of states in society. Solving these problems is not something theoretical definition itself can accomplish, but this paper hopes to provide a starting point for subsequent discussion and testing.

最后需要交代本文的自我定位。本文为一套理论建构型的整合分析框架，而非实证研究；在当前阶段，其核心评价标准为逻辑自洽性、跨场景解释力与立场一致性，而非量化精度、操作化指标或传统实证显著性。

Finally, the paper's self-positioning should be stated. This paper is a theory-building integrative analytical framework, not an empirical study; at the current stage, its core criteria of evaluation are logical self-consistency, cross-scenario explanatory power, and consistency of position, rather than quantitative precision, operationalized indicators, or traditional empirical significance.

本文不预设普世的价值结论，仅提供分析工具；所有规范性推论均建立在主体主动“勾选”的前提之上。

This paper presupposes no universal value conclusions and provides analytical tools only; all normative inferences are built on the premise of the subject's active "check."

文中涉及的经典理论均作为思想参照使用；本框架的整合路径为独立提出，未见直接对应的先例。

The classical theories touched upon in the text are all used as intellectual reference points; the framework's integrative path was proposed independently, and no directly corresponding precedent has been found.

最后交代本框架的生成顺序。本文的概念并非先在的哲学构造，而是对一批更早完成的具体材料的抽象化与去特征化总结：在本文写作之前，这些概念已在一系列叙事创作与对话文本中被实际使用——包括以为具体个体重新评估其过去、选择与经历之价值为形式的完整应用过程。例如，“人生支线”的论证结构最初来自一次为具体之人所作的辩护，其后才被公理化与普适化；又如，同一段为某个体写下的文本，曾在几乎不做改动的情况下适用于另一位处境迥异的对象，并保持了效力——这类跨案例的可迁移性，构成了本文将其抽象为框架的最初动机与信心来源。这些叙事创作在方法论上的地位接近于思想实验：具体应用涉及具体之人的具体处境，完整呈现将与本文“不提供人生内容”的自我约束相冲突，故案例的特征被刻意隐去。换言之，本文并非没有案例；框架先被使用，后被总结，本文交付的只是工具本身，工具的使用记录不在本文的范围之内。

Finally, the order in which this framework was generated should be explained. The concepts of this paper are not prior philosophical constructions. They are an abstracted and de-particularized summary of a body of concrete material completed earlier. Before this paper was written, these concepts had already been put to actual use in a series of narrative works and dialogic texts — including complete processes of application in the form of re-evaluating, for concrete individuals, the value of their pasts, choices, and experiences. For example, the argumentative structure of the "branch line of a life" originally came from a defense made for a concrete person, and was only later axiomatized and universalized. Likewise, a passage of text written for one individual was once applied, with almost no modification, to another person in an entirely different situation, and it retained its force. This kind of cross-case transferability constituted the initial motivation for abstracting these concepts into a framework, as well as the initial source of confidence in doing so. The methodological status of these narrative works is close to that of thought experiments: concrete applications involve the concrete situations of concrete persons, and presenting them in full would conflict with this paper's self-constraint of "providing no life content," so the identifying features of the cases have been deliberately removed. In other words, this paper is not without cases; the framework was used first and summarized later, and what this paper delivers is the tool itself — the records of the tool's use are not within the scope of this paper.

最后补充一点使用说明性质的态度声明。本文并不追求哲学或理论意义上的完备性：它不声称这套概念已经覆盖价值问题的所有面向，也不把“完备”作为自己的评价标准——不完备不等于不自洽，框架内部的概念相互衔接，但它无意成为封闭体系。本文提供的只是一套足够好用的工具，评判标准不是它是否完备，而是它能不能用、好不好用。至于为什么建议使用这一整套，而不是从各处摘取概念自行组装，理由是实际的：这套工具是集成的。全部概念在同一套词汇中定义，共享同一组前提，彼此之间的接口已经预先校准；使用者不必再亲自完成概念之间的整合工作，可以从任何一个概念进入，沿着已有的衔接走到其余部分。一个工具箱的价值不在于其中任何一件工具是终极的，而在于它们能够配合运转。

One final statement should be added, in the nature of a note on how to read this paper. This paper does not aim at philosophical or theoretical completeness. It does not claim that this set of concepts covers every aspect of the problem of value, and it does not take completeness as one of its own criteria — though incompleteness is not inconsistency: the concepts within the framework interlock, even as the framework has no intention of becoming a closed system. What the paper offers is simply a set of tools that are good enough to use, and the standard for judging them is not whether they are complete, but whether they work, and work well. As for why one should use this set as a whole rather than picking concepts from various sources and assembling a toolkit of one's own, the reason is practical: this set is integrated. All of its concepts are defined in a single vocabulary, share a single set of premises, and have had their interfaces calibrated in advance. The user does not need to do the integration work between the concepts personally, but can enter from any one of them and follow the existing connections to the rest. The value of a toolbox lies not in any of its tools being ultimate, but in their working together.
